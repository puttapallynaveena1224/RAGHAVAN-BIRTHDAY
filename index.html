THE PERSON BEHIND THE NAME

Raghavan — More Than Just a Friend 💙

A chapter written about someone truly special...

Raghavan ni describe cheyyalante, simple ga “friend” ani cheppadam chala takkuva anipisthundi. Endukante, naa life lo nuvvu just oka friend ga kaadu… oka manchi brother aipoyaru, mainly maa family member aipoyaru.

26 September 2025 — THANKS for calling me. Meeru naaku kakunda vere inkevariko call chesi unte, nenu mimmalni meet ayyedanni kadu… mukhyanga, oka manchi bro ni miss chesukune daanni.

Meeru chala manchivaru, Raghavan.

Inka meeru topper, full knowledge untadi. Edaina topic gurinchi meetho matladithe, conversation ayipoyaka kuda “ivala edho kothaga nerchukunna” ane feeling untundi.

Annitikante ekkuvaga naku nachhedi mee character.

Especially, girls patla meeru chupinche respect chala genuine ga anipisthundi. Respect anedi words lo cheppadam easy, kani behaviour lo chupinchadam important. Aa quality meelo chala natural ga untundi.

Kani annitikante special thing enti ante…

Nannu sister la treat chestharu. 💙

Adi naaku chala special and valuable. Nijaniki, aa adrustam andariki dorakadu anukunta. Vere vaalla life lo friend la kakunda family bond tho okarini fill cheskodam anedi vere level. So, maybe I AM LUCKY IN THAT anukunta. 💙

Konni rojulu aithe maa intlo naa kante meeke ekkuva preference vastundemo anipisthundi. 😂 Ala jarigithe I’m definitely going to be jealous of you, brohhh! 😂💙 Just kidding.

Kachitanga life lo meeru chala manchi position ki veltharu.

Some people enter our lives as friends…

but somewhere along the way, they become family. 💙

And for me, you're definitely one of those people, Raghavan. 🫶💙
